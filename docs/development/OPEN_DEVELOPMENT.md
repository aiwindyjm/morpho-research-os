# Open Development

We build Morpho in public. Public work must correspond to a real feature, fix, design decision, documentation change, research fixture, or community response.

**Now:** architecture and foundation. **Next:** planner and task engine. **Later:** knowledge graph and incremental research. Monthly milestones end with a usable demo or a documented decision; weekly releases happen only when a coherent, tested change is ready.

Share meaningful progress through GitHub Releases, a short changelog entry, and occasional screenshots or a 15–30 second demo. Do not manufacture commits, issues, stars, or PRs.
