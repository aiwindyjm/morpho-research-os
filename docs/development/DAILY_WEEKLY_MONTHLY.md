# Development Cadence

## Daily

Commit a real, reviewable unit: a decision, document, test, fixture, bug fix, or scoped implementation. Do not commit solely to create activity.

## Weekly or bi-weekly

Publish a release only when a coherent change is tested, documented, and demonstrable. A release may be skipped when no meaningful user-facing change is ready.

## Monthly

Close one milestone with a short narrative: what changed, what can be tried, what remains, and what feedback is needed. Update the roadmap based on evidence instead of dates.
