- Distribute builds of this software under the Morpho name or logo —

  name and branding, and make clear it is not the official release;
- Use the logo in your own product identity, merchandise, domain names,

## For distributors and fork maintainers

The build files under `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` and
`apps/desktop/public/icon.png` exist so the official project builds and
ships. If you distribute your own build, replace them with your own icons
before release. Keep the originals' copyright notice in your source tree.
